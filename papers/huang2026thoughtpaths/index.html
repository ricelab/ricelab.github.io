ThoughtPaths: Navigating Persistent, Multi-Perspective AI Trajectories in Collaborative Knowledge Work

Huang, Z., Ferraro, G., Okai-Ugbaje, S., Tang, A., and Andres, J. (2026). ThoughtPaths: Navigating Persistent, Multi-Perspective AI Trajectories in Collaborative Knowledge Work. In Proceedings of the 38th Australasian Conference on Human-Computer Interaction (OzCHI '26), 729--737.

Abstract

Most generative AI interfaces organise interaction as a linear conversational thread. However, open-ended collaborative knowledge work often requires comparing alternatives, revisiting ideas, and negotiating direction over time. We designed and studied ThoughtPaths, a multi-branch generative AI interface that produces three parallel response trajectories---balanced, critical, and provocative---and maintains them as persistent, navigable structures. Through a qualitative study with five groups (three dyads, two triads; N = 12), we examine how knowledge workers use these branches to expand and redirect exploration, surface assumptions through contrast, and coordinate decisions by revisiting earlier trajectories. We found that persistent divergence supported negotiated interaction while introducing challenges in managing cognitive load. This work contributes an open-source prototype, an empirical account of collaborative practices, and design implications for generative AI interfaces that support knowledge work.
